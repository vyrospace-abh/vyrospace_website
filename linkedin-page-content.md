## 6. Content Strategy — 4 Pillars

A complete LinkedIn presence needs a publishing strategy. Here are the four content pillars:

### Pillar 1: Portfolio Spotlights 🎨
- **Frequency:** 2x/week
- **Format:** Carousel or single image before/after
- **Copy template:** "Before & After: [Project type]. We transformed [client brief] into a photorealistic [service]. Swipe to see the process → #3DRendering #ArchViz"

### Pillar 2: Industry Insights 📊
- **Frequency:** 1x/week
- **Format:** Text post or short infographic
- **Topics:** "Why photorealistic 3D renders increase real estate sales by 30%", "Top 5 mistakes in architectural visualization", "Virtual staging vs. traditional staging"

### Pillar 3: Behind the Scenes 🏗️
- **Frequency:** 1x/week
- **Format:** Video or carousel
- **Content:** Render farm tour, artist workflow, revision process, timelapse of a project from wireframe to final render

### Pillar 4: Client Stories & Case Studies 📖
- **Frequency:** 1x/month
- **Format:** Long-form text + image or document PDF
- **Template:** "How we helped [client type] visualize [project] in [timeline]. Result: [outcome]. #3DVisualization #ProductVisualization"
