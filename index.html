03 — ABOUT

BUILT AROUND
THE DETAIL.
WE BUILD IMAGES
FROM DESIGN.

Vyrospace is a CGI and visual production studio specializing in architectural visualization and product visualization. We combine 3D, cinematography, lighting, materials and visual direction to turn designs into imagery that communicates more than technical drawings can.

Every visual starts with intentional composition, camera language and visual hierarchy — developing materials, lighting, reflections, scale and atmosphere to make each frame believable without compromising the original design.
